# Taka Green

Environmental conservation and waste sensitisation site for Nairobi. Inferred from the brief (no interview, by the user's request to plan then build). Assumptions are marked.

- Audience: Nairobi residents, students, estates and businesses. Assumption: mostly on phones, often outdoors.
- Job of the site: show that waste sorted in the right place becomes something valuable (recycling, green waste), and what happens when it is dropped in the wrong place.
- Reference: the "Tuna Taka Taka" bottle-cage billboard (a steel mesh billboard filled with plastic bottles).
- Tone: Nairobi street energy, bold signage, Swahili phrases, hopeful, not preachy.
- Mode: Persuade / Experience. Interactive 3D, scroll storytelling, a sorting game.
- Claims: no statistics are stated as fact unless verified. Illustrative uses of recycled plastic are labelled as examples.
